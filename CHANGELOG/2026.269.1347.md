Relocate the `check-docs` R10 bed (and the `docs-site-app` box it deploys) from
`opencharly/charly`'s `charly.yml` into `opencharly/layer-docs-site`, the repo
that OWNS the `docs-site` candy the bed tests.

**This is the fresh re-land of the closed `opencharly/layer-docs-site#3`**
(`feat/relocate-check-beds`, auto-closed by the validator's 5-unanswered-BLOCK
threshold), rebased onto current `main` with every outstanding finding fixed. The
auto-close policy requires a fresh branch; the predecessor branch is not touched.

WHY relocate: a check bed must live in the repo that owns what it tests. The bed
runner's dev-tree override (`proc.SelfSuperprojectOverridePair`,
`spec/proc/repo_override.go`) points the bed PROJECT's own superproject's
`@github` refs at the local tree. While the bed sat in `charly`, the `docs-site`
candy was never exercised as in-development code. Relocated here, the bed composes
the LOCAL candy with no `@github` pin.
